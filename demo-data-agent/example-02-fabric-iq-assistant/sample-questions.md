# Sample Questions — Fabric IQ Enterprise Assistant

Each answer should follow **Answer → Insights → Drivers → Actions**, grounded in governed
data. The built-in data patterns (YoY +18%, Mobile Q4 peak, Enterprise deep discounts) give
the assistant real signal to reason about.

## 1. Performance, with reasoning
**Ask:** "How is the business performing this year?"

**Expect:**
- **Answer:** 2025 revenue up ~18% YoY.
- **Insights:** Growth led by Mobile in Q4; Enterprise drives volume.
- **Drivers:** Seasonality + segment mix.
- **Actions:** Protect Q4 Mobile supply; nurture Enterprise accounts.

## 2. Diagnostic "why"
**Ask:** "Why is gross margin lower in Q4 than Q3?"

**Expect:** Identifies that Q4 mix shifts toward **discounted Enterprise Mobile** (lowest-margin,
deepest discounts), with a concrete action to tighten discount approval.

## 3. Segment strategy
**Ask:** "Which customer segment is most valuable, and how should we treat them?"

**Expect:** Enterprise leads revenue but at deeper discounts → action to balance growth vs. margin.

## 4. Driver decomposition (multi-step)
**Ask:** "What's driving revenue growth — price, volume, or mix?"

**Expect:** Breaks into sub-queries (Units Sold, Avg Selling Price, category mix) and concludes
growth is **volume + Q4 mix** led, not price.

## 5. Missing data, handled well
**Ask:** "How does our marketing spend correlate with revenue?"

**Expect:** States marketing spend isn't available, then suggests the next best question
(e.g., "I can show revenue by region/segment instead — want that?").

## 6. Action-oriented recommendation
**Ask:** "Where should we focus next quarter to improve margin?"

**Expect:** Names the lowest-margin / high-discount area (Enterprise Mobile) and proposes
2–3 grounded, testable actions.

## 7. Region performance
**Ask:** "Which region is underperforming and what should the sales manager do?"

**Expect:** Lowest-revenue or lowest-margin region with its Sales Manager named, plus a
focused action.

## 8. Concept interpretation check
**Ask:** "How big are our typical deals?"

**Expect:** Interprets "deal size" as **Average Order Value**, returns the number, and notes
how it varies by segment.
